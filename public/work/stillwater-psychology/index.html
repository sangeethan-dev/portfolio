Getting started

Starting therapy shouldn't feel like one more thing to figure out

Many people put off reaching out. Not because they don't need support, but because the first step feels unclear.

  • Who should I see?
  • What will it cost, and can I claim Medicare?

Our free 15 minute call answers all three before you commit to anything.

Ways to fund your sessions

Not sure what applies to you? We'll go through it on your free call.

  • Medicare

    Rebates are available with a GP Mental Health Treatment Plan. See your GP first for your plan and referral.

  • Private patients

    No referral needed. Book directly without a GP visit.

  • NDIS

    For self managed and plan managed participants.

  • WorkCover

    For approved WorkCover claims. Let us know your claim details when you book.

  • Private health insurance

    You may be able to claim through your extras cover, depending on your fund. You can't claim private health and a Medicare rebate for the same session.

  • Telehealth

    Video sessions from anywhere in Australia.

FAQ

Questions people ask before booking

Can't see your question? Ask it on your free call, or contact us using the details below.

Do I need a GP referral?

Not for the free call, and not if you'd like to see us as a private patient. To claim Medicare rebates, you'll need a GP Mental Health Treatment Plan and referral from your GP before your first session.

How much does a session cost?

A standard 50 minute session is $230. Extended 80 minute sessions are $320, and 80 minute couples sessions are $340. Medicare rebates are available with a GP Mental Health Treatment Plan.

How long is a session?

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Extended and couples sessions are 80 minutes, which gives more room for couples work or conversations that need extra time.

What happens on the free call?

A member of our team calls you at the time you book on Halaxy. We'll ask a little about what's bringing you in, answer your questions about fees and funding, and suggest the psychologist who fits best. There's no obligation to book a session.

Can I have sessions by telehealth?

Yes. We offer video sessions to clients anywhere in Australia. Many people find it easier to fit around work, school pickups or a new baby.

Do you see teenagers?

Yes, from age 12. James Okafor works with teens and young adults, including ADHD support. Early on, we talk with each family about how confidentiality works and how parents can stay involved.

Can you help during pregnancy or after having a baby?

Yes. Priya Nair works with parents from pregnancy through the early years. Priya also sees couples, which can help when a new baby changes things at home.

Do you work with NDIS participants?

Yes, if your plan is self managed or plan managed. We can talk through how it works with your plan on your free call.

What if I need to cancel?

We understand life happens. Please contact us as early as you can. We ask for 48 hours' notice, as late cancellations and missed appointments are charged the full session fee.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm, with Thursday evenings until 8pm.

I'm an existing client. How do I book?

Use our existing client booking page on Halaxy to choose your next appointment.

What if I need help right now?

We're not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 000. For support any time of day or night, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

Getting started

Starting therapy shouldn't feel like one more thing to figure out

Many people put off reaching out. Not because they don't need support, but because the first step feels unclear.

  • What will it cost, and can I claim Medicare?

Fees and funding

Clear fees, and help with what you can claim

In person and telehealth sessions cost the same. Fees are paid on the day, and Medicare rebates are processed on the spot.

Ways to fund your sessions

Not sure what applies to you? We'll go through it on your free call.

  • Medicare

    Rebates with a GP Mental Health Treatment Plan and referral.

  • Private patients

    No referral needed. Book directly without a GP visit.

  • Private health

    Claim through extras cover, depending on your fund. Not alongside Medicare for the same session.

  • NDIS

    For self managed and plan managed participants.

  • WorkCover

    For approved claims. Bring your claim number to your first session.

  • Reduced fee places

    A small number each week for students and concession card holders.

If you need to reschedule

Life happens. Please let us know as early as you can. We ask for 48 hours' notice, as late cancellations and missed appointments are charged the full session fee.

Reminders are sent by SMS two days before each appointment.
